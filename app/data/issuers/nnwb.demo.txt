Ways to pay
UPI: nnwb@okhdfcbank or nnwb@okicici
Net banking / NEFT: A/c 50100234567890, IFSC SBIN0001234 (State Bank of India, Hajiganj branch)
Cash: any Nirmal Nagar counter. Always take the printed receipt.

The Board does not accept transfers to any other account, and does not send payment links on WhatsApp.
Bills are issued on the 1st of the month and are due on the 20th. A late payment fee of 2% per month applies after the due date.
If your bill looks wrong, call the numbers printed on the bill, not a number from a message.
